## Branch control behavior — 2026-09-15

These refinements extend `sys-tenant-001`, `sys-branch-002`, `sys-waiter-001` and `sys-report-001` without adding a parallel requirement catalogue.

- The restaurant control center keeps Organization → Brand → Branch and the branch time zone visible. One accessible branch may be selected directly; multiple branches start in an explicit all-branch overview. Search and URL selection cannot bypass current membership, subscription, branch assignment or permission checks. Branch-specific actions require a selected authorized branch and never silently choose the first branch.
- Current service tasks remain separate from historical results. Pending drafts, ready positions, waiter calls, bill requests and open tables open the appropriate authorized waiter workspace. Unavailable counts or reports are not represented as zero; retained data shows its timestamp and stale state.
- Report presets are today, yesterday and the last seven calendar days, including today. Custom dates are inclusive and limited to 31 calendar days. One atomic `period` URL value contains either the preset or `custom:YYYY-MM-DD:YYYY-MM-DD`; Apply validates and commits the whole range, and browser history restores it together. Each branch's time zone determines its date boundaries. Counts, order amounts and payments retain their distinct meanings and currencies are never combined into a misleading total.
- Readiness presents current branch, tables, QR, guest-menu, routing, opening-hours and settings checks with named required, review and optional states. Links respect the relevant management capability. Current readiness and ordering state do not reset the persistent onboarding completion record.
- Temporarily pausing guest ordering requires an explicit branch-scoped, validated and authorized save. A failed save retains the editable draft. Refresh does not replace unsaved pause fields, and branch switching requires saving or explicitly discarding them. The displayed state distinguishes manual pause, scheduled closure and setup problems.
- Waiter attention is allowlisted to `all`, `pending`, `ready`, `calls` and `bills`. Branch, zone and open-session scope applies before resolving a selected table. A selected table on another page opens its matching bounded page when it satisfies the current attention filter; malformed, foreign or inaccessible identifiers fail closed.

Implementation boundaries are documented in [frontend](frontend.md), [architecture](architecture.md) and [accessibility](accessibility.md); fresh acceptance evidence belongs in [testing](testing.md).

## Recovery behavior — 2026-09-15

These refinements implement the existing guest, menu/media and staff requirements below; they do not introduce a parallel requirement catalogue.

- An explicit guest URL language may update a restored guest record only while that guest is active. UI language resolution never reactivates or changes the identity of a removed, departed, rejected or pending guest.
- When a configured dish becomes unavailable, hidden or removed, keep the open detail panel, comment, selected modifiers and request identity. Show the localized availability conflict, allow explicit refresh/retry, and prevent adding until the current server-authorized menu permits it. Closing must return keyboard focus to the detail trigger or, if it disappeared, the menu heading.
- Filesystem cleanup during rollback must not replace the persistence exception, interrupt remaining cleanup callbacks or leak callbacks into the next transaction. Attempt every owned new file; preserve existing referenced images. Ordinary deletion and committed-operation cleanup retain their explicit failure/retry behavior.
- A failed kitchen/bar transition clears earlier success feedback. Offline staff and guest mutation controls disable while the current data and local dismissal controls remain usable; reconnect restores the controls without implying that a mutation succeeded.
